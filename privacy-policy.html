Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DEYVANTA Technologies (“DEYVANTA”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website.

2. Information you provide

If you contact us through a linked social platform or another communication channel, that platform may process the information you submit. This website currently does not provide an on-site account registration or payment form.

3. YouTube content and third-party services

This website may display public video information, such as video titles, thumbnails, publication dates and links, retrieved through the YouTube Data API. When you choose to open a video, you leave this website and use YouTube, which is operated by Google. Google may collect information according to its own privacy policy and settings. We do not control third-party services.

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and analytics

We do not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies through the current website code. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may send technical request information to Google when those fonts load. Cloudflare, our hosting or security provider, may process limited technical information such as IP address, request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website. Browser or third-party services may also use their own technologies.

5. How information is used

Information processed by hosting or security providers may be used to operate, maintain, troubleshoot and protect the website. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data retention and security

We do not maintain a user account database on this website. Technical logs, if generated by service providers, are retained according to their own policies. We take reasonable steps to keep website configuration and API credentials secure; API keys used by the site are configured on the server and are not intentionally published in the page source.

7. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. External links

Our website may link to YouTube, Instagram and other third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website features or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our Contact page or contact us through the official DEYVANTA social profiles linked on this website.

This page is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. Update it if you add forms, analytics, advertising, payments, accounts or other data collection features.
